HEPTA Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

1. Local-First Data Storage

HEPTA is architected with a strict privacy-first, local-first philosophy. All of your tasks, notes, routines, and milestones are stored securely on your local device using an encrypted database.

We do not transmit, sync, or backup your tasks to any external servers without your explicit manual action. Your data belongs to you.

2. Biometric Vault Security

The HEPTA Vault utilizes Android's native Biometric API (Fingerprint/Face Unlock). Your biometric data never leaves your device's secure hardware element and is completely inaccessible to the HEPTA app itself. It is used strictly to verify your identity locally.

3. Calendar Integration

If you choose to grant Calendar permissions to HEPTA, the app reads your calendar events strictly locally to display your daily load and schedule on the grid. We do not store, modify, or transmit your calendar data externally to our servers or any third parties.

4. Third-Party Tracking

HEPTA is built to be a focused environment. We do not use any third-party analytics, ad-tracking, or telemetry SDKs. We do not sell your usage data because we do not collect it.

5. Data Deletion

Because HEPTA is a local-first application, you have complete control over your data. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data through your Android system settings will permanently and instantly delete all your information. Please ensure you have manually backed up your data if you intend to keep it.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy or your data, please contact the developer team at Zincstate.
